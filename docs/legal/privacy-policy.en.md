## 2. What data we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work. You enter most of it yourself; some is recorded by the app as you use it.

### 2.1 Account and profile
- email address and password (the password is held by the sign-in service in a form we cannot read);
- if you sign in with Google: the data Google passes for sign-in (email address and account identifier);
- full name, nickname (shown on your public Šapa pomoći profile), city, interface language;
- account type (pet owner, shelter or breeder);
- time and version of the Terms of Use you accepted;
- technical account states: whether the trial has been used, whether account deletion was requested and when it takes effect, whether you are suspended from Šapa pomoći.

### 2.2 Subscription and payment
- plan (Premium or breeder, monthly or yearly), subscription status, trial and paid-period end dates, whether auto-renewal is on;
- identifiers assigned by Paddle (customer, subscription, transaction);
- card brand and last four digits — for display only;
- a record of every charge attempt (amount, currency, success or failure).

**We never receive or store your card details.** Payments are handled entirely by Paddle (see section 4).

### 2.3 Pets and the health record
- pet details: name, species, breed, sex, date or year of birth, neutered status, colour and markings, microchip number, passport number, photo, note, weight unit;
- your vet's name and phone number (if you enter them);
- weight, vaccinations (name, date, next dose, clinic, batch), treatments and doses given, vet visits (reason, findings, recommendations, cost, follow-up) and visit attachments (images or PDFs up to 10 MB);
- feeding and stool log;
- "moments" (photos and short text) and, if you turn them on, public links to them;
- memorial details (date, reason, note);
- for every entry — which user made it.

Data about animals is not personal data in itself, but it is when linked to you as the owner. Your vet's name and phone number are data about another person — enter them only if that is appropriate.

### 2.4 Reminders and notifications
- reminders and their settings (types, delivery time, channels);
- a delivery record of each reminder sent (channel, time, outcome, the provider's message id, error description);
- for push notifications: the subscription address generated by your browser, its encryption keys and a browser description (user agent);
- for WhatsApp (Premium, if you turn it on): your phone number and the time you verified it. We do not store the verification code.

### 2.5 Household
- household membership, join date, whether you receive reminders for shared pets;
- invitations: the invitee's email address (if sent by email) and the invitation status. Invitation links are stored only in a form that cannot be turned back into the link.

### 2.6 Organisations (shelters and breeders)
- organisation details: name, city, tax ID (optional), registration note, description, contact phone, email and website, logo, donation details (bank account, payment link, drop-off address and hours);
- team members, their roles (Admin or Volunteer) and team invitations (email address and role);
- the organisation's animals, intake (date, arrival type, place found), status history, litters;
- **adoption requests and breeder inquiries:** the applicant's name, phone, email and message;
- record transfers to a new owner: the recipient's email address and the transfer status.

### 2.7 Partners
- when you tap "call", "send message", "send email" or "visit website" on a partner, we record the partner, the action, the time and your account;
- your current location is used only if you allow it in your browser, only to compute distances to partners, and is not stored. Without permission we use your profile city.

### 2.8 Šapa pomoći
- listings: type, title, description, category, species, city, price (selling only), photos (up to 5), publish and expiry dates, outcome;
- messages between users and when a message was read;
- users you have blocked;
- reports: reason, note, status and outcome;
- a Šapa pomoći suspension, if the Operator imposes one.

### 2.9 Data export
- when you request an archive of your data, we keep a record of the request (status, size, dates) and the archive itself until it expires (section 7).

### 2.10 Technical data
- short-lived records that prevent the same request being processed twice and that limit how many actions can be taken in a time window (e.g. invitations or messages);
- technical logs of the hosting service, which may include your IP address and browser description.

**We use no external analytics or advertising tools** and do not build marketing profiles.

## 5. What is public and what other users see

- **Public moment link** (if you turn it on): anyone with the link sees the photos, text and pet name. The link is unguessable, you can turn it off, and it stops working when you delete the moment.
- **Šapa pomoći:** active listings (photos, title, description, city, price, date) are visible to everyone, even without signing in. Your public profile shows your nickname (or your name if no nickname is set), city, member-since date and active listings. **Your email address and phone number are never shown on your public profile.**
- **Messages** in Šapa pomoći are seen only by the two people in the conversation. The Operator does not read messages; for a report it sees only the reason and the note written by the reporter.
- **Household:** members see the pets the owner shares, all their entries and who made each entry; the owner and members see each other's email addresses.
- **Organisation team:** members see the organisation's animals, adoption requests and inquiries (with the applicant's name, phone and email) and the other members' email addresses.
- **Adoption request or breeder inquiry:** your name, phone, email and message go to the organisation you send it to. That organisation decides independently on the adoption or sale and is responsible for how it uses your data afterwards.
- **Public shelter and breeder pages:** show the organisation's details and public details of its animals (never the health record). Donation details are shown only to signed-in users.
- **Record transfer:** when an organisation hands a record to a new owner, the organisation keeps a read-only copy.
- **The Operator** (us, as the app's administrator) has access to the data needed to approve shelters, manage partners and moderate Šapa pomoći.

## 7. How long we keep it

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Your account and all account data | until you delete your account |
| Account deletion | the account is deactivated at once; final deletion follows **30 days** after the request. Within those 30 days you can sign in and cancel the deletion — the account is restored unchanged. After that, database rows and files are deleted (no later than day 31). |
| Database backups | **14 days** (so deleted data may remain in a backup for at most 14 more days; backups are used only to recover from a failure) |
| A pet you delete | immediately from the database; its files in the nightly cleanup |
| Memorial pets | until you delete them (no automatic deletion) |
| Reminder delivery records | **12 months** |
| Household and team invitations | valid for 7 days, then marked expired |
| Record transfer links | valid for 30 days |
| Data export archive | **7 days** after it is ready, then deleted |
| Šapa pomoći listings | active for 30 days (you can extend them); expired and resolved listings stay in your listings until you delete them |
| Conversations and messages | until you or the other party delete your account; they remain if the listing is deleted |
| Short-lived technical records (duplicate-request protection, rate limits) | 24 hours, or until the end of the rate-limit window |
| Payment data held by Paddle | as required of Paddle as the seller (accounting, tax) |
| Hosting service technical logs | [TO BE CONFIRMED] |

What remains after your account is deleted:
- entries you made in **someone else's** pet record (as a household or team member) stay with that owner, without the information that you made them;
- if you own a household, the household and its shared pets pass to the earliest-joined member (if they have an owner account and no household of their own); otherwise the household is deleted;
- if you are an organisation's last member, the organisation and its animals are deleted together with your account;
- reports you filed stay with the Operator for moderation, without the information that you filed them;
- a record an organisation handed over to a new owner stays with the new owner.

## 11. Data security

- Database access is limited by row-level rules: you see only your own data and what is shared with you.
- Files are private and open only through temporary links valid for 10 minutes; the only public files are photos of moments with a public link turned on and photos of active listings.
- The connection to the app is encrypted (HTTPS); the session cookie is not readable by scripts in the browser.
- Invitation links are stored only in one-way hashed form; links in reminders are signed and time-limited.
- Passwords are held by the sign-in service; we never see them.
- The database is backed up every night.

If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the Commissioner and, where required, you (Art. 52 and 53 of the Law).

## 12. Your rights

Under the Law you have the right to:

- **be informed** about processing and **access** your data (Art. 26);
- ask for **rectification** of inaccurate data (Art. 29) — you can edit most data yourself in the app;
- ask for **erasure** (Art. 30) — in the app: Profile → My data (Moji podaci) → "Delete account" (with a 30-day period to change your mind);
- ask for **restriction** of processing (Art. 31);
- receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (**portability**, Art. 36) — in the app: Profile → My data (Moji podaci) → "Download all my data". The archive contains a `data.json` file with your data plus your files; we email you when it is ready and it is available for 7 days;
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 37);
- **withdraw consent** at any time where processing is based on consent (e.g. location or push permission), without affecting earlier processing;
- lodge a **complaint with the Commissioner** for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (Bulevar kralja Aleksandra 15, 11000 Belgrade, www.poverenik.rs) (Art. 82).

We answer requests without undue delay and within 30 days of receipt at the latest (Art. 21). We may ask you to confirm your identity first — usually by sending the request from your account's email address.
