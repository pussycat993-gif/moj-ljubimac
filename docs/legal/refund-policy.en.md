# Refund Policy — Moj Ljubimac

**Last updated:** [PUBLICATION DATE]

## 1. Who sells you the subscription

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller **Paddle.com**. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders — Premium (monthly and yearly) and the breeder plan (monthly and yearly). Paddle charges you, handles VAT, issues the invoice and **handles all refunds**. Paddle's Buyer Terms also apply to your purchase ([LINK TO PADDLE BUYER TERMS]); where this policy and Paddle's terms differ in your favour, the more favourable rule applies.

## 2. Trial

Premium has a one-time 7-day trial. Nothing is charged during the trial. If you cancel renewal before the trial ends, you are not charged. The breeder plan has no trial.

## 3. 14-day refund

3.1. You can ask for a full refund within **14 days of the date of a charge**, without giving a reason. This applies to the first charge (after the trial, or straight away if you have no trial) and to each automatic renewal. [TO BE CONFIRMED — renewals too, or the first charge only.]

3.2. If you are a consumer in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have a statutory right to withdraw from the contract within 14 days of purchase. This policy gives you at least as much.

3.3. After a refund the subscription ends and the account moves to the Free plan (existing data stays visible; see Terms of Use, section 3.5).

## 4. After 14 days

After the 14 days we do not refund a period that has already started, in full or in part. Instead you can **cancel renewal at any time**: there are no further charges, and Premium or the breeder plan stays active until the end of the period already paid for.

We do refund after 14 days in these cases:
- **duplicate purchase** — if you buy the same subscription twice by mistake, we cancel the second one at once and refund it;
- a charge after cancellation caused by a system error;
- any other case where the law requires it.

## 5. How to ask for a refund

- Through Paddle: reply to the receipt email Paddle sent you or use Paddle's buyer support page ([LINK — e.g. paddle.net]); or
- write to us at [KONTAKT MEJL] with your account email and the date of the charge — we will pass the request to Paddle.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Timing depends on your bank or card issuer, usually [5–10] business days.

## 6. Cancelling a subscription

- In the app: Profile → "Cancel renewal", or Profile → "Manage billing" (Paddle's portal).
- Cancelling does not by itself refund money; if you also want a refund within the 14 days, request it as described in section 5.
- Deleting your account cancels renewal automatically; if you cancel the deletion within 30 days, renewal resumes. Deleting your account does not automatically refund the current period. [TO BE CONFIRMED]

## 7. Failed payments

If a renewal charge fails, Paddle retries and notifies you by email. A failed charge takes no money, so there is nothing to refund.
